# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Everyone taking part in issues, pull requests, and other project spaces is
expected to follow it.

## In short

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Keep discussion focused on the work.
- No harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour breaks this code, report it using GitHub's
[Report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
option on the comment, issue, or pull request, or by mentioning the maintainer,
@mikesilvers, in the relevant thread.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide, or delete comments, lock conversations, limit
interactions, or block contributors who break this code, and will act in line
with the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
